                Let's turn on your camera

                Point it at the places where you put things down, not at yourself: tilt the screen back or put the laptop to one side. Close is better; keys should look at least as big as a fingertip in the picture. Video stays on this computer. iCamFind only keeps small photos of the things themselves.

                Show me your places

                Drag a box over each place where you put things, then give it a name. Keep the boxes on the surfaces, away from where you sit: things are remembered once they're fully inside a box. Add the door too if you can see it, and iCamFind will remind you if you leave without your keys.
